1. Geographical Location and Characteristics
The protected area investigated is the zone of Nizhne-Ivkino, located in the Kumenksky District of the Kirov Region, Russia. 
The nearest official protected area is the Nurgush State Nature Reserve (founded in 1994), which protects floodplain complexes of the Vyatka River and old-growth forests. The Nizhne-Ivkino zone acts as a recreational area near these natural complexes. The area is characterized by mixed and coniferous forests, which are vital for the local ecosystem.
2.1 Rare Species of Plants and Animals
Based on the Red Data Book of the Kirov Region, the following endangered species inhabit the forests and wetlands of this area:
Endangered Plants (Flora)
Venus’s slipper (Cypripedium calceolus) - An endangered orchid species.
Calypso bulbous (Calypso bulbosa) - A rare plant found in dense forests.
Bird’s-nest orchid (Neottia nidus-avis) - Grows in shady coniferous forests.
Siberian pine (Pinus sibirica) - A valuable tree species.
Marsh fragrant orchid (Gymnadenia conopsea) - Found in wetlands and meadows.
Endangered Animals (Fauna)
White-tailed eagle (Haliaeetus albicilla) - A bird of prey.
Lesser spotted eagle (Aquila pomarina) - An endangered bird.
Barn owl (Tyto alba) - A nocturnal hunter.
European beaver (Castor fiber) - Keeps the wetlands clean.
Garden dormouse (Eliomys quercinus) - A small mammal living in trees.
Apollo (Parnassius apollo) - A large butterfly.
2.2 habitats of endangered plants and animals
Plants:
Venus’s slipper (Cypripedium calceolus) — it grows in damp but not swampy places in mixed and deciduous forests; on the slopes of above-floodplain terraces with calcium-rich soil.
Calypso bulbous (Calypso bulbosa) — it likes dense, shady coniferous and mixed forests with moss cover; it avoids clearings and open areas.
Bird’s-nest orchid (Neottia nidus-avis) — it lives in shady coniferous forests, often on rich forest litter.
Siberian pine (Pinus sibirica) — in the Kirov Region, this tree is mostly an introduced or rare planted species.
Marsh fragrant orchid (Gymnadenia conopsea) — it can be found in wet meadows, forest edges, lowland swamps, and damp clearings.
Animals:
White-tailed eagle (Haliaeetus albicilla) — it builds nests near large water bodies and uses tall trees for nesting.
Lesser spotted eagle (Aquila pomarina) — it lives in mixed forests and hunts in open areas like meadows, clearings, and floodplains.
Barn owl (Tyto alba) — this bird is rare in the region; it likes old buildings, farms, abandoned houses, and open spaces for hunting.
European beaver (Castor fiber) — it lives along rivers, streams, and oxbow lakes where there are trees and bushes on the banks.
Garden dormouse (Eliomys quercinus) — it inhabits old deciduous and mixed forests, parks, and gardens with hollow trees.
Apollo (Parnassius apollo) — it prefers dry, sunny meadows and forest edges with calcareous soil; its caterpillars feed on stonecrops.

3. Environmental Threats
The ecosystem of the Kirov Region, including the Nizhne-Ivkino area, faces several significant threats 
Threat
Description
Habitat Loss
Deforestation and illegal logging destroy the natural homes of animals and plants 
Pollution
Air, water, and soil pollution affect the quality of the environment. Factories and power stations emit harmful gases 
Illegal Hunting
Poaching reduces the population of rare animals, breaking the natural balance 
Tourism Pressure
The high number of visitors to the sanatorium can damage the local nature if not managed correctly 

4. Environmental Vocabulary 
Endangered species – Animals or plants that might die out.
Natural habitat – The place where an animal or plant naturally lives.
In danger – In a risky situation; at risk of dying out.
Survive – To stay alive.
Wetlands – Land that is covered with water (e.g., marshes).
Pollution – The action or state of making or being made dirty or poisonous (air, water, soil).
Toxic fumes – Poisonous gas emitted by factories.
Acid rain – Rain mixed with acids that damages trees and lakes.
Conservation – Protecting animals and plants from being harmed.
Preserve – To keep something safe and protected.
Ecosystem – A community of interacting organisms and their physical environment.
Volunteer – A person who works without pay to help people or the environment.
Recycle – To process used materials (like cans or paper) so they can be used again.
Collect rubbish – To pick up waste and throw it away properly.
Protect – To keep someone or something safe from harm.
Solar power – Energy from the sun (an environmentally friendly energy source) 
Species extinction – When a whole species of animal or plant dies out.
Plant flowers/trees – To put plants in the ground to help nature.
Natural environment – Nature as it exists without man-made changes.
Toxic – Poisonous; very harmful.
Habitat destruction – The process by which a natural habitat becomes incapable of supporting its native species.
5.resent news
Recent environmental improvements in Nizhne-Ivkino:
Hydro-unit repair on the Ivkina River. In 2021, the hydro-unit was repaired to protect the local ecosystem and prevent habitat destruction. The work included updating water-release structures and cleaning the pond bed near the beach — this helped preserve the natural environment and reduce pollution risks.
Cleanup events. Locals took part in the “Silver Spring” cleanup (subbotnik) to collect rubbish around the spring and nearby paths. These actions help protect wetlands and natural habitats and support conservation efforts.
Eco-friendly improvements. The town improved walking zones, added bins, and created recreation areas. This reduces harm to the ecosystem, encourages people to respect nature, and supports a healthy natural environment.
