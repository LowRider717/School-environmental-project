Route guide

How to reach
the springs

Use the pedestrian route from the Nizhne-Ivkino sanatorium to the mineral springs near the Ivkina River.

Walking route

Sanatorium to mineral springs

  1. Start at the Nizhne-Ivkino sanatorium.
  2. Follow the path east toward the Ivkina River.
  3. Continue to the rotunda and the marked mineral springs.

The springs are on the left bank of the river, below the bridge near the settlement.
