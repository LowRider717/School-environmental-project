Endangered species

Animals or plants that might die out.

Natural habitat

The place where an animal or plant naturally lives.

Wetlands

Land covered with water, such as marshes.

Conservation

Protecting animals and plants from harm.

Ecosystem

A community of organisms and their physical environment.

Pollution

The action of making air, water or soil dirty or poisonous.

Habitat destruction

The process by which a natural habitat can no longer support its native species.

To process used materials so they can be used again.
